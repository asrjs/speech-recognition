## 2024-06-25 - Avoid Nullish Coalescing Operator in Tight DSP/Inference Loops
**Learning:** V8 incurs measurable overhead when evaluating nullish coalescing operators (`??`) in tight, hot loops iterating over TypedArrays. When array bounds are known to be safe, standard typescript assertions (`!`) eliminate this branching penalty. Furthermore, mathematical equivalencies in hot paths (like tracking local maximums for log-sum-exp stabilization) should be strictly pruned; simplifying `bestValue - (bestValue + Math.log(expSum))` to `-Math.log(expSum)` shaves significant CPU cycles.
**Action:** In ML inference or DSP loops, prioritize absolute mathematical simplicity and avoid non-null checks when the underlying structure is a dense `Float32Array` representing a known tensor shape. Use `!` to satisfy TypeScript instead of runtime fallbacks.
