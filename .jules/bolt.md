## 2024-03-24 - TypedArray access nullish coalescing performance penalty
**Learning:** In V8 environments (Node/Bun/Chrome), using the nullish coalescing operator (`??`) inside tight inner loops dealing with TypedArrays (like Float32Array) significantly degrades performance compared to using the non-null assertion operator (`!`). A microbenchmark on a 10,000-element Float32Array showed `??` taking ~390ms vs `!` taking ~158ms for an equivalent iteration loop. The fallback handling adds measurable overhead compared to direct unsafe/asserted access.
**Action:** When working in hot execution paths with Array-like objects or TypedArrays where the bounds and lengths are statically known to be safe (such as in `argmax` or logit loops), prefer using the non-null assertion operator (`!`) to preserve performance and avoid the runtime penalty of `??`.
